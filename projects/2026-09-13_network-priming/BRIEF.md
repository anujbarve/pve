# Network Priming — How One Word Activates Your Entire Mental Web

## Hook & Core Mystery
You read the word "doctor." A split second later, the word "nurse" becomes easier to recognize. You didn't choose this. Your brain automatically lit up an entire network of related concepts — and you never felt a thing.

## Shot List
1. **Shot 01: The Flash Word (The Hook)**
   - *Narration:* "You see the word 'doctor' for three hundred milliseconds. You don't even consciously register it. But your brain already knows what's coming next."
   - *Environment:* A dark void. A single word — "DOCTOR" — flashes in golden typography, then vanishes. In its afterimage, faint ghost-words flicker: NURSE, HOSPITAL, STETHOSCOPE, PRESCRIPTION. They pulse once and fade.

2. **Shot 02: The Spreading Activation (The Fracture)**
   - *Narration:* "One word triggers a cascade. 'Doctor' activates 'nurse,' which activates 'hospital,' which activates 'ambulance,' which activates 'emergency.' The entire semantic network lights up like a city grid at dusk."
   - *Environment:* A network graph with "DOCTOR" at the center. Golden activation waves ripple outward through connecting nodes. Each node briefly glows gold as the wave reaches it, then settles to a warm amber. The activation spreads in concentric rings — one word igniting dozens.

3. **Shot 03: The Semantic Distance Map (The Engine)**
   - *Narration:* "Words closer in meaning activate faster. 'Nurse' lights up almost instantly after 'doctor.' 'Butterfly' takes longer. Your brain has a map — and proximity is measured in meaning, not spelling."
   - *Environment:* A topographic map where words are plotted as peaks. "Doctor" and "nurse" sit on adjacent peaks, connected by a short golden bridge. "Butterfly" sits on a distant peak, connected by a long, dim path. The activation wave travels faster across short bridges.

4. **Shot 04: The Implicit Bias Machine (The Glitch)**
   - *Narration:* "Priming doesn't just affect word recognition. Primed with 'old,' people walk slower. Primed with 'achievement,' they perform better on tests. Your behavior follows the network — whether you notice or not."
   - *Environment:* A figure walks across a stage. Words flash subliminally above — "SLOW," "ELDERLY," "WEAK" — and the figure's gait visibly changes. The words switch to "FAST," "YOUNG," "STRONG" and the figure accelerates. The environment warps around the primed state.

5. **Shot 05: The Awareness (The Synthesis)**
   - *Narration:* "You are not the author of every thought you have. Some thoughts were placed there by the last word you read, the last image you saw, the last conversation you overheard."
   - *Environment:* Wide cinematic pull-back. The network graph glows fully — thousands of interconnected nodes, all pulsing with ambient activation. The figure stands in the center, connected to everything. Manifesto typography: "YOUR MIND IS A NETWORK — AND EVERYTHING PRIMES IT."
