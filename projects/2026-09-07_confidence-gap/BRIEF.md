# The Confidence Gap (Dunning-Kruger Effect)

## Core Narrative Arc
1. **The False Summit (The Hook):** A person stands atop a tiny peak, surveying the world with absolute certainty — unaware of the massive mountain behind them.
2. **The Valley of Doubt (The Collapse):** The ground crumbles as real knowledge reveals how much they didn't know — confidence plummets into the valley of despair.
3. **The Ascent (The Climb):** Slow, grinding progress through uncertainty — each step earned through failure and doubt.
4. **The Real Peak (The Mastery):** Reaching genuine expertise — not with arrogance, but with quiet confidence born from knowing how deep the rabbit hole goes.
5. **The Synthesis (The Reframe):** The peak and the hill are both real — the gap isn't between smart and stupid, it's between knowing enough to know what you don't know.
