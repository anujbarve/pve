# The Sunk Cost Fallacy — Why You Can't Walk Away From a Bad Bet

## Hook & Core Mystery
You've waited two hours in a restaurant line. The food arrives — and it's terrible. You eat every bite. Not because you're hungry, but because you already invested two hours. Your brain treats time spent as currency — and refuses to let you burn it.

## Shot List
1. **Shot 01: The Two-Hour Meal (The Hook)**
   - *Narration:* "You waited two hours. The food is terrible. You eat every bite anyway — not because it's good, but because you already invested the time."
   - *Environment:* A cinematic close-up of a plate of food under harsh restaurant lighting. The food visibly deteriorates — textures go wrong, colors dull. But a fork continues to lift food to an unseen mouth. A timer in the HUD reads "2:00:00" and counts up.

2. **Shot 02: The Investment Ledger (The Fracture)**
   - *Narration:* "Every dollar, every hour, every effort you've already spent is gone. It's not coming back. But your brain keeps a running ledger — and it demands a return on investment."
   - *Environment:* A massive golden ledger book fills the frame. Numbers accumulate in the "INVESTED" column — dollars, hours, emotional energy. The "RETURN" column stays stubbornly empty. The ledger glows with increasing urgency as the invested column grows.

3. **Shot 003: The Concorde Trap (The Engine)**
   - *Narration:* "The British and French governments kept funding the Concorde for decades after it became economically irrational — because they'd already spent so much they couldn't stop."
   - *Environment:* A sleek supersonic jet silhouette (Concorde-like) flies through a dark sky. Below it, a trail of burning currency and clock faces streams behind. The jet's engine sputters, but the funding stream continues to pour in. The plane refuses to land.

4. **Shot 04: The Escalation Ladder (The Glitch)**
   - *Narration:* "The sunk cost fallacy doesn't just keep you in bad investments — it makes you pour more in, chasing the losses with increasing desperation."
   - *Environment:* A ladder that extends downward instead of upward. Each rung glows gold as a foot steps on it. The ladder gets longer, the rungs get darker, but the figure keeps climbing down. The ground is nowhere in sight. The descent accelerates.

5. **Shot 05: The Walking Away (The Synthesis)**
   - *Narration:* "The money is gone. The time is gone. The only real choice is whether you waste tomorrow too."
   - *Environment:* Wide cinematic pull-back. The figure steps off the ladder. The ladder crumbles behind them. They walk forward into open, clear space. The ledger book slams shut. Manifesto typography: "WHAT'S SPENT IS SPENT — WALK AWAY."
