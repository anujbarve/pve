# Anchoring Bias — The First Number Hijacks Everything

## Hook & Core Mystery
A stranger mentions the number 65. You don't know why. Minutes later, when asked what percentage of African nations belong to the UN, your answer is wildly higher than someone who heard the number 10. The first number anchored your entire judgment — and you never even noticed.

## Shot List
1. **Shot 01: The Number Drop (The Hook)**
   - *Narration:* "A stranger tells you the number sixty-five. It seems random, meaningless — just a number floating in the air."
   - *Environment:* A dark void. A single golden number "65" materializes in the center, glowing with warm light. It pulses once, then radiates anchor tendrils downward like golden roots, embedding itself into the ground plane.

2. **Shot 02: The Judgment Warp (The Fracture)**
   - *Narration:* "Minutes later, you're asked to estimate something completely unrelated — and your answer is anchored to that random number like a ship tied to the ocean floor."
   - *Environment:* A horizontal scale line stretches across the frame. A golden anchor chain descends from above, pulling a judgment dial toward the anchored number. The dial resists, but the chain is relentless.

3. **Shot 03: The Neural Mechanism (The Engine)**
   - *Narration:* "Your brain doesn't reset between questions. It takes whatever number is most recent and uses it as the starting line for every estimate that follows."
   - *Environment:* A neural pathway fires from a number node through synaptic connections, each synapse lighting up with the same golden hue. The number persists, glowing, while new information tries to enter but gets deflected by the anchor's gravitational pull.

4. **Shot 04: The Real-World Damage (The Glitch)**
   - *Narration:* "Negotiate a salary, and the first number on the table sets the entire range. Your dream salary becomes a negotiation against someone else's anchor."
   - *Environment:* Two salary figures — one gold, one gray — sit on opposite sides of a negotiation stage. The gold number pulls the gray one toward it, warping the space between them. Price tags, house listings, and courtroom settlements flash as ghostly overlays.

5. **Shot 05: The Liberation (The Synthesis)**
   - *Narration:* "The anchor isn't magic — it's a shortcut. Knowing it exists is the first step to cutting the chain."
   - *Environment:* Wide cinematic pull-back. The golden anchor chain shatters. The number 65 dissolves into particles. The scale line resets to its true center. Manifesto typography emerges: "THE ANCHOR ISN'T REAL — CUT THE CHAIN."
