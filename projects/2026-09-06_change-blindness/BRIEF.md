# Brief: Change Blindness (The Illusion of Seeing Everything)

## Hook & Core Mystery
You are giving directions on the street. A large wooden door passes between you, silently swapping the person with a total stranger. You keep talking for minutes without noticing because your brain only tracks 2 degrees of foveal reality and fakes the rest.

## Shot List
1. **Shot 01: The Door Experiment (The Street Swap)**
   - *Narration:* "You're giving directions to a stranger on the sidewalk, when two workers carry a wooden door right between you—swapping the person with a total stranger."
   - *Environment:* Ambient urban street sidewalk at twilight. Direction vectors between two silhouettes. A wooden construction door glides across the visual axis, swapping Stranger A into Stranger B.

2. **Shot 02: The Perceptual Fracture (The Blind Conversation)**
   - *Narration:* "Over half of all people never notice—continuing the conversation for minutes without realizing they are talking to an entirely new face."
   - *Environment:* Close observation stage with active mismatch HUD. Discrepancy vectors highlight mismatched physical features (height, jacket, voice) that bypass awareness.

3. **Shot 03: The Optical Constraint (The 2-Degree Fovea)**
   - *Narration:* "Your eyes don't record a high-definition world. Razor-sharp vision is confined to just two tiny degrees at the center—the width of your thumbnail at arm's length."
   - *Environment:* Spatial field of view diagram emerging from an eye profile. Crisp 2° golden foveal cone cutting through a blurred atmospheric matrix.

4. **Shot 04: The Neural Reconstruction (Faking the Periphery)**
   - *Narration:* "Everything outside that narrow spotlight is a low-resolution prediction, continuously filled in by memory and expectation."
   - *Environment:* Dynamic neural simulation sweep. Wireframes and predictive textures aggressively painting over raw blurred visual inputs in real time.

5. **Shot 05: The Synthesis (The Minimal Summary)**
   - *Narration:* "You don't see reality as it exists—you see a fleeting summary of what your brain assumes is still there."
   - *Environment:* Wide cinematic pull-back coalescing foveal optics, predictive neural fields, and physical reality into a unified manifesto composition.
