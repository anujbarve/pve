# Predictive Processing (v2 - Reality First)

## Narrative Arc
1. **Human Experience Hook:** Reaching for a phone that isn't there (Reality-first).
2. **The Camera Myth:** Pulling back from reality into the wrong-way wiring diagram.
3. **The Mechanism:** The prediction vs. sensory error mismatch happening in real-time.
4. **The Implication:** The brain patching a typo in real life (overriding eyes).
5. **The Payoff:** The world as a wireframe of expectations solidifying.

## Narration Script
**Frame 1:** You reach for your phone, expecting it to be exactly where you left it. But it's moved. For a split second, reality glitches.
**Frame 2:** We used to think perception worked like a camera. Light enters the eye, and the brain builds a picture. But the physical wiring goes the wrong way.
**Frame 3:** Your brain doesn't wait to see the world. It projects a guess. Your senses don't tell your brain what is there. They only report the errors.
**Frame 4:** If the guess matches the input, no information travels up. You only process surprises. If the expectation is strong enough, your brain literally overrides your eyes.
**Frame 5:** You aren't experiencing objective reality. You are experiencing your brain's expectation of reality, constantly corrected by the world.
