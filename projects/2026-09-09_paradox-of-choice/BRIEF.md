# The Paradox of Choice — Why More Options Make You Miserable

## Hook & Core Mystery
You stand in front of a wall of 200 jams. You taste thirty. You buy none. The person who saw six jars walked out with two and felt great. More choice didn't free you — it paralyzed you.

## Shot List
1. **Shot 01: The Wall of Jars (The Hook)**
   - *Narration:* "Two hundred jams on a wall. You taste thirty. You walk out with nothing. Meanwhile, someone who saw six jars bought two and felt perfectly satisfied."
   - *Environment:* A massive wall of colorful jar silhouettes stretches across the frame. The camera pushes in slowly as jars multiply, crowding the visual field. A single hand reaches toward the wall — then retreats.

2. **Shot 02: The Decision Freeze (The Fracture)**
   - *Narration:* "Barry Schwartz called it the paradox of choice. Past a certain point, more options don't increase satisfaction — they increase regret."
   - *Environment:* A decision tree branches violently from a single trunk, splitting into dozens then hundreds of thin golden paths. Each path glows briefly, then fades. The branches crowd each other, creating visual noise. A figure stands at the fork, frozen.

3. **Shot 03: The Opportunity Cost Machine (The Engine)**
   - *Narration:* "Every option you choose means all the others become ghosts of what you didn't pick. Your brain runs a simulation of the road not taken — and it never stops."
   - *Environment:* A central chosen path glows gold while rejected paths shimmer as ghostly outlines. Each ghost path projects a brief "what if" visualization — a phantom life, a phantom meal, a phantom outcome. The ghosts multiply and press inward.

4. **Shot 04: The Maximizers vs. Satisficers (The Glitch)**
   - *Narration:* "Maximizers chase the best possible option. Satisficers pick one that's good enough. Maximizers make more money, choose better — and are profoundly less happy."
   - *Environment:* Split composition. Left side: a figure on a hamster wheel, chasing an ever-receding golden star (the Maximizer). Right side: a figure sitting contentedly on a hillside, the golden star resting beside them (the Satisficer). The contrast is spatial and emotional.

5. **Shot 05: The Liberation (The Synthesis)**
   - *Narration:* "The secret isn't finding the best option. It's choosing one and never looking back."
   - *Environment:* Wide cinematic pull-back. The wall of jars shrinks to a single, elegant jar. The decision tree collapses to one clean path. Manifesto typography: "CHOICE IS FREEDOM — UNTIL YOU CHOOSE TO CHOOSE."
