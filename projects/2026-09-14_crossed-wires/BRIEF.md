# Crossed Wires — When Your Senses Bleed Into Each Other

## Hook & Core Mystery
You hear a C-sharp and see a flash of crimson. You taste the number seven — it's metallic, slightly sweet. For a small percentage of humans, the boundaries between senses aren't boundaries at all. They're bridges.

## Shot List
1. **Shot 01: The Tasting Number (The Hook)**
   - *Narration:* "For one in every two thousand people, the number seven tastes like copper. Wednesday is salmon-colored. A G-major chord is bright orange."
   - *Environment:* A dark field. The number "7" appears in golden typography. As it materializes, a copper-colored mist blooms from its surface. The mist takes on a metallic sheen, dissolving into abstract taste-representations. The number itself shimmers with color.

2. **Shot 02: The Crossed Board (The Fracture)**
   - *Narration:* "In a typical brain, the visual cortex, the auditory cortex, and the gustatory cortex are separate neighborhoods. In synesthesia, the walls between them are thin — or missing entirely."
   - *Environment:* Three distinct neural regions are shown as separate rooms in a building. Normal brains have solid walls. The synesthetic brain has doors left open, light bleeding from one room into the next. Golden wires cross between the rooms, carrying signals across boundaries.

3. **Shot 03: The Remapping Engine (The Engine)**
   - *Narration:* "Synesthesia isn't a disorder — it's a different wiring of the same hardware. The brain's pruning process simply left more connections alive than usual."
   - *Environment:* A neural pruning animation. Young neurons with excess connections (shown as a dense web) are selectively trimmed in a typical brain. In the synesthetic brain, certain connections survive — specifically between adjacent sensory regions. The surviving connections glow gold.

4. **Shot 04: The Creativity Boost (The Glitch)**
   - *Narration:* "Synesthetes are disproportionately represented among artists, musicians, and poets. Kandinsky heard colors. Pharrell tastes sounds. The crossed wires aren't noise — they're extra bandwidth."
   - *Environment:* A split composition. Left: a painter's canvas that responds to music — colors ripple with each note. Right: a musical score where notes have visible colors. The two sides mirror each other, connected by a golden bridge of synesthetic crossover.

5. **Shot 05: The Universal Connection (The Synthesis)**
   - *Narration:* "Maybe synesthesia isn't the anomaly. Maybe it's a reminder that your senses were never meant to be separate — and that the world is richer than any single sense can capture."
   - *Environment:* Wide cinematic pull-back. All five senses are represented as overlapping translucent circles — sight, sound, taste, touch, smell — their intersections glowing gold. Manifesto typography: "YOUR SENSES ARE NOT ISLANDS — THEY ARE A CONTINENT."
