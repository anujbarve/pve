# Project Brief: The Spotlight Effect (Nobody is Looking at You)

## Core Insight & Mystery
You walk into a cafe with a coffee stain on your shirt, feeling a 10,000-watt spotlight on your chest. In reality, everyone's gaze vectors are aimed 180 degrees inward into their own private anxiety bubbles.

## Shot Breakdown & Timings

1. **Shot 01: The 10,000-Watt Stain (0.00s – 9.62s)**
   - *Audio*: "You walk into a crowded cafe with a coffee stain on your shirt, feeling a blinding ten-thousand-watt spotlight burning directly into your chest."
   - *Visual*: Warm cafe atmosphere suddenly dominated by an intense, harsh conical light beam burning down onto a macro fabric weave with a dark coffee drop. The surrounding cafe falls into stark dramatic contrast.
   - *Action*: Lens pushes in macro toward the glowing stain as anxiety telemetry meters jump.

2. **Shot 02: The Hallucinated Audience (9.62s – 17.64s)**
   - *Audio*: "In your mind, every pair of eyes in the room instantly locks onto your flaw, silently dissecting your embarrassment."
   - *Visual*: A perspective cafe scene where harsh red/amber ocular laser rays project from every background customer silhouette, converging onto your chest with high-frequency targeting reticles.
   - *Action*: Parallax camera drift across the converging gaze vectors, highlighting the overwhelming perception of collective scrutiny.

3. **Shot 03: The Barry Manilow Test (17.64s – 28.70s)**
   - *Audio*: "In landmark Cornell experiments, subjects wearing humiliating shirts predicted fifty percent of the room noticed—yet barely twenty-three percent ever even glanced."
   - *Visual*: Scientific archival slate presenting the 2000 Gilovich et al. Cornell study. Dramatic side-by-side split data visualization: Perceived Scrutiny (50%) bar glowing hot amber vs. Actual Notice (23%) bar shrinking into cold factual steel.
   - *Action*: Animated data transition with dynamic metric counter and visual crowd partitioning.

4. **Shot 04: The Inward Vectors (28.70s – 37.84s)**
   - *Audio*: "The truth is nobody is watching you. Every human being around you is trapped in their own private anxiety bubble, obsessing over their own flaws."
   - *Visual*: Overhead isometric spatial cafe view with ambient tables and glowing figures. With a dramatic audio whoosh, all laser vectors snap 180 degrees inward, pointing into their own translucent thought spheres ("My deadline", "Did I sound stupid?", "My haircut").
   - *Action*: Dynamic camera crane reveal showing that zero rays are aimed outward.

5. **Shot 05: The Blackout of Judgment (37.84s – 46.42s)**
   - *Audio*: "When you realize the spotlight is only an internal projection, you don't overcome the audience—you realize the audience was never there."
   - *Visual*: Wide cinematic resolution. The harsh spotlight abruptly powers down into warm, calm ambient lighting. The projection grids dissolve into pure space. Hero manifesto typography seamlessly emerges in the physical environment: "THE AUDIENCE NEVER EXISTED. YOU ARE FREE."
   - *Action*: Cinematic camera pull-back into warm golden equilibrium.
