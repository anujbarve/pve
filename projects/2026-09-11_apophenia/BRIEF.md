# Apophenia — Your Brain's Addiction to Patterns That Don't Exist

## Hook & Core Mystery
You see a face in the clouds. A message in the static. A conspiracy in coincidences. Your brain isn't broken — it's doing exactly what evolution designed it to do: find patterns, even when there are none.

## Shot List
1. **Shot 01: The Face in the Static (The Hook)**
   - *Narration:* "You stare at television static and see a face. It's not there — but your brain insists it is, with the same conviction it uses to recognize your own mother."
   - *Environment:* A field of TV static fills the frame. A faint face-like pattern emerges in the noise — two eye-like shapes, a mouth-like shadow. The camera slowly pushes in as the face becomes clearer, then shifts focus to reveal it's just random pixel noise.

2. **Shot 02: The Pattern Detection Machine (The Fracture)**
   - *Narration:* "Your visual cortex is a pattern-matching machine so aggressive it would rather see a predator in the bushes fifty times wrong than miss it once."
   - *Environment:* A neural circuit diagram where signal lines branch and reconnect. False connections flash gold momentarily — false positives — while the system keeps running. A "PATTERN DETECTED" alert pulses in the HUD overlay.

3. **Shot 03: The Pareidolia Engine (The Engine)**
   - *Narration:* "Pareidolia — seeing faces in toast, Jesus in water stains, meaning in randomness — isn't a bug. It's the same neural wiring that lets you recognize a friend across a crowded room."
   - *Environment:* A rapid montage of ambiguous shapes — a cloud formation, a wood grain, a coffee stain, a rock formation — each briefly resolving into a face-like pattern before dissolving back to abstraction. The transitions are fluid, one morphing into the next.

4. **Shot 04: The Conspiracy Spiral (The Glitch)**
   - *Narration:* "Once your brain finds a pattern, it can't unsee it. Connect the dots hard enough and you'll find meaning between any two points — even if the dots are random."
   - *Environment:* Dots scattered across a dark field. Golden lines begin connecting them, forming increasingly complex networks. The connections multiply until the entire frame is a web of golden threads — a conspiracy board. Then the camera pulls back to reveal the dots were randomly placed.

5. **Shot 05: The Epistemic Humility (The Synthesis)**
   - *Narration:* "The brain that sees patterns everywhere is the same brain that discovered fire, built civilizations, and wrote symphonies. The price of genius is a little bit of beautiful madness."
   - *Environment:* Wide cinematic pull-back. The conspiracy web dissolves into a constellation — the same connections, reframed as art. Manifesto typography: "YOUR BRAIN IS A PATTERN MACHINE — LEARN WHEN TO TRUST IT."
