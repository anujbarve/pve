## Visual Metaphors

- Memory = glowing neural nodes
- Forgetting = nodes dimming, connections fading
- Spaced repetition = a pulse that reignites fading nodes at the critical moment
- The forgetting curve = an exponential decay SVG that transforms into a staircase of retention
- Cramming = a flood of nodes all lighting up at once, then dying
- Spacing = precise pulses at the edge of decay, each one making the connection stronger
