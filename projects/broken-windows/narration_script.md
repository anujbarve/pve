# Narration Script — Broken Windows Theory

## Frame 01 — Hook
"You walk past a broken window every day. You don't even notice it. But what if I told you that one broken window could change everything?"

## Frame 02 — Origin
"In nineteen eighty-two, two criminologists named Wilson and Kelling noticed something interesting. They called it the Broken Windows Theory."

## Frame 03 — Mechanism
"Here's the idea. A broken window isn't just damage. It's a signal. It tells everyone that nobody is watching. Nobody cares."

## Frame 04 — Cascade
"And once that signal goes out, things spiral. One broken window. Then two. Then ten. Then the whole block falls apart."

## Frame 05 — Reframe
"But here's the thing. It was never about the window. It's about the signal it sends."

## Frame 06 — Lesson
"The bigger lesson? The small things you ignore are telling everyone what you'll tolerate. Fix the signal. Change the story."
