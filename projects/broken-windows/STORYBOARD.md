# Broken Windows Theory — The Signal

**This video tells the vertical-feed scroller that a broken window isn't just damage — it's a social signal that invites more disorder, and the real lesson is that small things signal big things.**

Silent build: `music: none`, no `SCRIPT.md`. No spoken narration — every frame carries its message through progressive typographic reveals paced across the frame's duration. Editorial Dark Mode per `Anuj-style.md`; visual system per `frame.md` (broadside preset, remixed: ink-black = off-white `#F2F1EC`, cream = black `#000000`, fire-orange = mustard `#F2D24B`).

## Video direction

The whole video shares one look and one motion grammar; per-frame Scene lines carry only the delta.

- **Palette system** — full-bleed `cream` (black `#000000`) ground on every frame; `ink-black` (off-white `#F2F1EC`) is the default reading color; `cream-muted` (gray `#8A8A8A`) for setup lines, attributions, and struck/echo text; `fire-orange` (mustard `#F2D24B`) is the **single accent**, allowed on exactly one focal per frame (never more); `border-dark` (light `#D9D9D7`) 1px hairline rules for load-bearing dividers. Type by role: **display** = Playfair Display 700 for punch/key/lesson lines, **display italic** = Playfair Display Bold Italic for emotional leads, **body** = Inter 400/500 for setup lines, kickers, labels. Case: display text is sentence/title case.
- **Motion grammar + reveal model** — long-tail `power3` settes (smooth over bouncy; `back`/`bounce`/`elastic` banned). Every frame follows the **beat-paced reveal** model: at t=0 only the frame's setup enters; every further line/word reveals on its own beat spread across the **back ~50%** of the frame; never front-load the whole canvas in the first quarter. Reveals are **progressive gray→white** (setup muted gray, then the statement resolves to off-white) with the **yellow accent landing last** on the focal.
- **Rhythm / held-frame allocation** — two deliberate stillness beats for rhythm: **frame 02 (origin card)** and **frame 06 (lesson card)** are allocated-stillness titlecards (calm reveal, long hold); frames 01, 03, 04, 05 are kinetic type beats. No frame front-loads; the held frames are the breathers that carry the piece.
- **Chrome (every frame except 02)** — swipe pill top-right (small round-pill, white arrow); `anujb.sh` signature bottom-left with thin rule; 1px hairline dividers separate setup from punch. Frame 02 (origin card) intentionally omits the swipe.
- **Negative list** — off-brand textures/decorative bokeh; default-AI purple-blue gradients; real browser chrome/cursors/nav; both motion failure modes: slideshow (front-load then freeze) and screensaver (many elements floating independently).
